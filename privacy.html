Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Countdown Chameleon ("the app") is designed with privacy in mind. This page explains what information the app does — and does not — collect.

What We Collect

Countdown Chameleon does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. We do not have a backend, database, or analytics service connected to the app.

What Is Stored, and Where

All countdown data you create — destination names, dates, themes, and any optional details like team names, school names, or baby names — is stored locally on your own device using your browser's or app's local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it
  • Is not accessible to us, to Apple, or to any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by removing countdowns in the app, or by clearing the app's data through your device settings

Sharing Feature

If you use the app's Share feature, an image and/or a link containing your countdown's details (destination, date, theme) may be sent through your device's native share sheet — for example, via Messages, Mail, or other apps you choose. This sharing happens entirely through your device's own share functionality; we do not receive or store a copy of anything shared.

Backup & Restore

The app's optional Backup feature lets you export your countdown data to a file saved on your own device. This file is never uploaded anywhere by the app — it is yours to keep, move, or delete as you choose.

Third-Party Services

Countdown Chameleon does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all — all data stays on-device as described above.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hunterappstudio@gmail.com, or via the support page.
